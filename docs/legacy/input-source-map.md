## Investigation boundary

Provider documentation and targeted authenticated calls establish offered fields, definitions, sampled periods and current access. Every family below has a disposition. **Supported** means supported within the stated sample and contract, not every issuer. **Limited** identifies a demonstrated missing field, basis, period or entitlement. **Primary evidence** identifies a company/public-source path and its applicability conditions. An untested company is not a proven absence. Per-company acquisition and route checks must retain unsupported coverage and reasons rather than inventing zeros or changing the method.

The source requirement is separate from a calculation rule or judgment. This investigation does not need to classify every peer, extract every issuer's notes or choose later route interpolation thresholds. It establishes the finite observation families those operations consume. A later route that needs another dataset, history window or basis must reopen its source assessment.

## Account assignment and capability blockers

FMP remains the primary financial-data source. GuruFocus remains required for the demonstrated modern security-class preflight and is assigned the exact moat/Z″/Beneish and current ADR-ratio gaps for later consumers. A configured key does not imply access to every endpoint: preserve the tested FMP402 refusals. Public BLS/OECD/reference studies and primary issuer documents are separate source families, not fictional FMP or GuruFocus fields.

The decisive limitations are the absent accepted earnings/forecast-basis reconciliation in the checked feeds (FT-012/013/022), incomplete capital/lease context and historical rate evidence (FT-012/014/015/016/018), historical share/ADR and availability metadata (FT-003/004/022), and the tested document/holder access and metadata restrictions (FT-007/033/039). Each blocker is conditional on the missing input for the company/period; primary evidence can resolve some of them. Neither sufficient does not mean every valuation or every company is blocked.

No method was removed, no missing observation became zero, and no gross acquisition-price or commercial-paper-turnover requirement was invented to obtain this verdict. Unsupported cutoffs, company populations and documents remain unsupported with reasons. Founder decisions D2–D9 settle the source families; later formulas and per-company judgments do not authorize silently expanding the evidence claims.

## Exit decision

**AQ-11 / RA-01 investigation complete: neither inspected vendor feeds/current accounts is sufficient for the full required set.** This completes the planning investigation with capability blockers; it does not certify universal usable coverage or waive the release's runtime/acceptance checks. Implement the authorized R1 source assignment above and retain partial/gap outcomes. The founder's release acceptance reviews this verdict alongside the real ten-period statement and earnings-evidence flow.
